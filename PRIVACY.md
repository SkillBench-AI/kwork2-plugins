# Kwork Privacy

Kwork is opt-in. Each client collects only after sign-in and explicit
organization and repository authorization, and sanitizes on the device before
anything is uploaded.

| Client | Notice |
|---|---|
| Kwork for Claude Code | [Privacy notice](docs/privacy/claude-code.md). A disclosure draft pending approval by the SkillBench privacy/legal owner. |
| Kwork for Codex | No separate notice yet. What it collects, how it is sanitized and how to stop it are described in [Data and privacy](clients/codex/plugins/kwork/README.md#data-and-privacy). A notice for Codex needs the privacy/legal owner. |

Both clients follow the [SkillBench Privacy Policy](https://skillbench.com/privacy/)
and the [SkillBench subprocessor list](https://skillbench.com/subprocessors/).
For privacy and data requests, contact `privacy@skillbench.com`; if your
organization provides Kwork, it may be the data controller and may need
to handle the request.
